01 — About

Engineering with intent, not just output.

2022

Where it started

Wrote my first real program and got hooked on the moment an idea turns into something that runs.

2023

Full-stack & systems

Started building complete products — frontend, backend, and the infrastructure that holds them together.

2024 — Now

AI & scale

Focused on applied AI, distributed systems, and shipping software that holds up under real usage.

I build software because I like the moment something goes from an idea to something real — a system someone actually uses, a problem that stops being a problem. That moment is the whole reason I do this.

My path into engineering wasn't a straight line — it was curiosity that kept compounding. Every project taught me to care less about "does it work" and more about "does it hold up." That shift changed how I build: cleaner architecture, fewer shortcuts, more ownership.

I think in systems. I care about the seams — where the frontend meets the backend, where a model meets a product, where good design meets good engineering. Those seams are where most software breaks, and where I spend most of my attention.

What's next: I want to work on problems with real stakes — systems people rely on, at a scale where craftsmanship actually matters. I'm looking for a team that builds like that.
